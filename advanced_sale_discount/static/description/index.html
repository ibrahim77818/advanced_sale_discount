Advanced Sale Discount

Give a percentage or a fixed amount off a whole quotation or customer invoice, and choose how it lands: spread on the lines, weighted by you, booked on one discount line per tax group, or driven by volume tiers. Totals are exact to the cent and follow the order all the way to the journal entry.

Why this module

Standard Odoo discounts work line by line. Customers negotiate a discount on the deal.

In standard Odoo 19

  • A global discount of “$ 100 off” must be worked out by hand into a percentage on every line.
  • Two-decimal percentages leave cents of difference between what was agreed and what is invoiced.
  • A negative line added by hand gets the wrong taxes when the order mixes VAT 15% and VAT 0% items.
  • No way to put more discount on strategic lines and less on low-margin ones.
  • Volume and contract discounts need pricelist rules per product and cannot look at the whole order.
  • A manual discount line is invoiced in full on the first partial invoice, even if most goods are not delivered yet.

With this module

  • Click Apply Discount, type a percentage or an amount, preview it, apply it.
  • The amount is split with a largest-remainder method; any leftover cent goes on a Rounding Adjustment line.
  • Dedicated Discount Line mode creates one line per tax group, with the group’s taxes and fiscal-position mapping.
  • Manual Weighted mode: a weight, a percentage or an amount per line.
  • Smart Tiered Volume: quantity or amount tiers per line or per document, with contract customers and validity dates.
  • Discount lines are invoiced in proportion to delivery; the last invoice settles the remainder exactly.

How it works

Four steps from quotation to journal entry.

1
Enable
Turn on Advanced Sale Discounts per company and pick the discount service product.
2
Choose
On a quotation or draft invoice, click Apply Discount and pick a mode and value.
3
Preview
See the new discount and amount per line before anything is written. Errors show in the wizard.
4
Invoice & post
The discount flows to (partial) invoices and posts with the native tax engine.

Features

Screens below use one demo quotation for Deco Addict: 3 × Office Chair at 100.00 and 7 × Desk Pad at 33.33 (VAT 15%), plus 1 × Conference Table at 466.69 (VAT 0%), for an untaxed amount of $ 1,000.00.

Menus & settings

One switch per company

Sales › Configuration › Settings › Advanced Discounts. When Advanced Sale Discounts is on, a Sale Discount Product is required. It is checked on save: it must be a service invoiced on ordered quantities, belong to the company (or be shared), and have an income account. Volume rules live in Sales › Configuration › Volume Discount Rules.

Feature 1

Proportional: $ 100 off, spread on value

The Apply Discount button sits in the quotation header. With Proportional and a Fixed Amount of 100.00, each line gets its share of the value and the wizard previews the New Discount (%) and Discount Amount. An existing line discount is kept and combined: 5% then 10% gives 14.5%.

Feature 2

Exactly $ 900.00 after applying

Line discounts are written, the optional Global Discount (%) column shows the extra percentage per line, and the chatter logs the mode and amount. The Global Discount tab keeps the mode, value, target and total discount for audit.

Feature 3

Manual Weighted: more discount where you choose

With By Weight, each line’s share is its value × its Weight. Here the Office Chair has weight 2, so it receives $ 46.15 of the $ 100.00. Percentage per Line and Amount per Line let you type the discount of each line directly.

Feature 4

Rounding Adjustment lines close the last cents

Percentages are stored with two decimals, so 15.3833% becomes 15.38% and the lines reach $ 99.97 instead of $ 100.00. With Post Rounding Difference on a Discount Line, one small line per tax group brings the untaxed amount to exactly $ 900.00, with the right VAT on each cent.

Rule: the requested amount is split in cents with the largest-remainder method, so the shares always add up to it. After writing the line discounts, the module compares the achieved and requested discount per tax group; the difference (here $ 0.02 on VAT 15% and $ 0.01 on VAT 0%) is posted on the discount product.
Feature 5

Dedicated Discount Line per tax group

Product lines stay at list price. A 10% discount adds Global Discount (VAT 15%) for $ -53.33 and Global Discount (VAT 0%) for $ -46.67, so VAT is reduced only where VAT was charged. Price-included taxes and fiscal positions are handled, and a shared analytic distribution is copied to the discount line.

Feature 6

Smart Tiered Volume and contract discounts

A Volume Discount Rule has tiers (Minimum Threshold → Discount (%)), evaluated Per Line or on the Whole Document, on Quantity (in the product unit of measure) or Untaxed Amount (in company currency). It can be limited to Contract Customers, Products, Product Categories (with sub-categories), validity dates and a company. The wizard shows which Volume Rule each line reached.

Priority: rules with contract customers are evaluated first, then by sequence. For each line, the first rule whose tier is reached wins. Leave Volume Rules empty in the wizard to use all active rules, or pick some.
Feature 7

Partial invoices carry their share of the discount

Order S00045: 10 × Installation at 100.00 (invoiced on delivered quantities) with a 10% dedicated discount line. After 4 are delivered, the invoice contains the discount line with quantity 0.40: $ 400.00 − $ 40.00. The next invoice takes 0.60, and the total invoiced equals the order. Line discounts and their origin are copied to the invoice too.

Feature 8

Discounts visible in the general ledger

Discount lines post to the income account of the discount product. Give it a Sales Discounts account and the discount appears as its own debit, while revenue stays at list price. Proportional, weighted and tiered discounts reduce each product’s revenue instead.

Feature 9

Fully translated to Arabic

Every field, mode, message and screen is translated (i18n/ar.po), and the wizard works right-to-left. A .pot template is included for other languages.

Built for real-world corner cases

Handled in the code, and most of them pinned down by the automated tests.

Re-applying
The previous global discount is removed first, so applying 10% twice stays 10%, never 19%.
Remove Discount
Restores each line’s original discount and deletes discount lines, also on confirmed orders.
Lines changed afterwards
Changing a product, quantity, unit, price or tax, or adding or removing a line, raises an out-of-date banner. The order cannot be confirmed, nor the invoice posted, until the discount is applied again or removed.
Locked or invoiced orders
Once an order is Locked or partly invoiced, its discount is frozen; a further discount can be applied on the draft invoice and removed there independently.
Invalid input
Zero, negative, over 100% or above the order value is refused; the wizard shows the reason before you click Apply.
Excluded lines
Sections, notes, zero-value lines, down payments, shipping and reward lines are never discounted.
Multi-currency
Amounts are rounded in the document currency; tier amount thresholds are converted to company currency at the document date.
Units of measure
Quantity tiers compare in the product unit: 2 dozen reach a 24-unit tier.
Duplicated quotations
Copies keep their discount and can be reset on their own.
